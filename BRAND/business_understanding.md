# Business understanding

Maverick Minds looks like one company but runs four businesses. Each has a different buyer, different economics and a different relationship to trust. The strategy has to be built around that fact.

| Engine | Who pays | Economics | What it needs from the brand | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Education** _(offline programmes → LEAP)_ | Aspiring exporters, business owners, colleges | One-to-many and scalable once online; price-competitive market | Proof that the teacher knows the *current* reality, not 2019's | Pulled into the "earn from exports" hype market, which damages everyone else's trust |
| **Advisory** _(MSME consulting)_ | Existing and export-ready MSMEs | High ticket, limited by founder time, relationship-led | Visible depth: specific, correct, sector-aware thinking | Founder bandwidth; hard to scale |
| **Incentive & scrip desk** | Two sides: exporters (sellers) and importers (buyers) | Transactional; thin spread; needs speed, trust and counterparties | Reliability and discretion, *not* reach | Scheme continuity, budget cuts, shrinking BCD base, and conflict of interest if mixed with commentary |
| **Intelligence** _(latent today)_ | No one yet | Cost centre now; the reputation engine for the other three; possibly a paid tier later | Accuracy, a consistent cadence, a track record | One badly wrong call on a policy detail |

[INTERPRETATION] The key insight: **intelligence is the only content type that is valuable to every one of these buyers at once.**

- The MSME uses it.
- The importer cares about it because it moves their duty bill.
- The beginner is impressed by it: it shows them the teacher is operating in the real market.

Beginner education content, by contrast, repels experienced exporters. Scrip promotion repels everyone who isn't transacting that week. So intelligence becomes the brand's spine. Education and services hang off it.


### Home turf

Maverick Minds is based in Coimbatore, according to its public website and the founder's public LinkedIn profile. That puts it inside one of India's densest export-manufacturing belts:

- Tirupur knitwear, Karur home textiles and Erode turmeric
- Coimbatore pumps, motors and castings
- Namakkal eggs, Ambur and Vellore leather, and Tuticorin marine products

National intelligence voices are Delhi-centric and national in lens. A credible **cluster lens** ("what this means for Tirupur") is an asset Maverick Minds can own without pretending to be national on day one.


### The five strategic risks

1. **Founder bandwidth.** The founder cannot be researcher, writer, consultant, trainer and deal-maker. The research system (Parts 10–13) exists to take research and first drafts off his plate.
2. **Accuracy risk.** An intelligence brand can be destroyed by one confident, wrong claim about a notification. The approval workflow (Part 15) is designed around this.
3. **Hype contagion.** If LEAP's marketing borrows the "lakhs per month from exports" register of the mass-market trainers, it undercuts the intelligence brand.
4. **Policy dependency.** See the scrip note above.
5. **Conflict of interest.** Commenting on RoDTEP while earning on RoDTEP transfers is legitimate only with disclosure and separation.
