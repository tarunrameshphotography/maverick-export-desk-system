# Brand voice guide

**The character:** a senior export manager who also reads the Gazette. Unhurried, specific, commercially minded. He explains the way you'd explain to a smart client across a desk, not to a classroom.

| Dimension | Setting | In practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authority | High on facts; measured on interpretation | State facts flatly, with the source. Mark interpretation as interpretation. |
| Informality | Low–medium | First person ("I read the notification so you don't have to" is fine). No slang, no emoji in LinkedIn text, no exclamation marks. |
| Opinion strength | Clear, bounded | "I'd price October orders as if RoDTEP is zero" is good. "The government is destroying exporters" is off-brand. |
| Sentence style | Short declaratives; one idea per line; concrete nouns | Name the notification, the HS code, the port, the date. Avoid abstractions like "the landscape", "the ecosystem". |
| Numbers | Precise and sourced | Always give unit, period and source ("₹10,000 crore, FY27 allocation, Union Budget"). Round only when you say so. |
| Politics | Neutral on parties; candid on policy mechanics | Critique the design of a rule, never a party or a person. |


### The uncertainty ladder

Uncertainty is stated with fixed words, so readers learn the scale:

- **Confirmed** — in a notified document.
- **Reported** — credible media, not yet notified.
- **Likely** — our read, most evidence points one way.
- **Possible** — a scenario worth preparing for.
- **Unclear** — we don't know, and here is what would tell us.


### Headlines and hooks

**Hook philosophy:** the first line states a specific consequence, not a feeling. Specificity is the hook.

- **Not this:** 🚨 BIG UPDATE for exporters!! Nobody is talking about this…
- **This:** From 1 October, your bank gives you 15 months to realise an export, not 9. Here's what else changes in the new FEMA rules.
- **Not this:** India–UK FTA is a game-changer for Indian exporters!
- **This:** UK duty on Indian knitwear is now zero. Against Bangladesh, that changes nothing. Here's where it does matter.
- **Not this:** Hormuz crisis: exporters face huge losses 😟
- **This:** If you're quoting CIF to a Gulf buyer this month, you're underwriting war-risk you can't price. Switch the Incoterm, not the buyer.

These examples illustrate voice only. Each needs the verification noted in the 30-day plan before it runs as real copy.


### Post anatomy (intelligence posts)

1. **Consequence line** — who is affected, and how.
2. **What changed** — two or three lines of fact.
3. **What it means** — interpretation, labelled as such.
4. **Who should care** — HS codes, markets, clusters.
5. **What to do this week** — one to three actions.
6. **What we're watching** — the next trigger, with a date if possible.
7. **Source line** — document, number, date.


### Vocabulary


#### Use

notified · in force · effective from · landed cost · realisation · rules of origin · margin of preference · exposure · HS line · buyer ask · window · watch for · the fine print says


#### Avoid

game-changer · huge opportunity · must-know · hidden secret · nobody is talking about · guaranteed · passive income · explode · skyrocket · "Let that sink in" · "Agree?" · 🚀💰🔥


#### Never, without a source

tariff rates · scheme rates or deadlines · trade values · quotes · company names in any negative context · "government is planning…"


### Corrections policy (part of the voice)

Mistakes get corrected in public: edit the post, add a first comment starting "Correction:", and log it in the Ledger. An intelligence brand that corrects itself visibly is *more* trusted, not less.
