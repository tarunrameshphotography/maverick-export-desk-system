> **CHALLENGE · SHOULD LEAP TARGET A NARROWER AUDIENCE?**
>
> **Yes.** The mass beginner market already has scaled incumbents with hundreds of thousands of followers, large student counts and aggressive pricing. Competing there means adopting their register, which contradicts the intelligence brand.
>
> The underserved buyer is the **owner of an existing business who has a product and wants the first export done right**. They will pay more, finish the course, become exporters, and later need advisory and incentive services. They also generate scrips.
>
> LEAP's promise should be concrete: *"In eight weeks, go from 'my product could sell abroad' to a costed, compliant, buyer-ready export plan, with your first qualified buyer conversations under way."* No income claims, no guaranteed buyers.
>
> Keep "Earn" in the name, but define it as *earning your first export order properly*, never as an income promise.


### Journeys, not one funnel

The brief proposes one path: Research → Content → Attention → Trust → Community → LEAP → Consultation → Scrip services. [OPINION] **That path is not logical for most people.** Scrip *sellers* are existing exporters. They will never come through a beginner course. Scrip *buyers* are importers, a different audience again. And LEAP buyers are mostly not advisory clients. Four journeys share one trust core instead:

- **Exporter journey** _(MSME exporters, experienced exporters)_ — Desk post → Follow + WhatsApp channel → Saturday Brief → "Ask the Desk" / 20-min diagnostic → Advisory / Incentive Desk → LEAP for teams
- **Builder journey** _(export-ready businesses, entrepreneurs, serious aspirants)_ — Instagram explainer / Headline vs Reality → Free "Export Reality Check" live session → Readiness checklist → LEAP cohort → First shipment → Joins exporter journey (and generates scrips)
- **Importer journey** _(BCD payers)_ — Direct outreach + CHA referral → Duty and FTA posts (proof of competence) → Scrip purchase → Duty / FTA advisory
- **Campus journey** _(colleges, students)_ — Institution partnership → Campus LEAP → Students follow the Desk → Future employees and exporters (long horizon)

[INTERPRETATION] The loop worth noticing: builder-journey graduates who start exporting become exporter-journey clients and scrip sellers. That makes LEAP a long-term supply channel for the other businesses, which is the real reason to keep it serious.


### Journey notes per audience named in the brief

| Audience | Entry point | What builds trust | Natural next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Complete beginners | Instagram reality-check and scam-awareness reels | Honesty about effort and risk | Free live session → LEAP Foundation only if they have a product or trade |
| Existing exporters | A Countdown or Fine Print post that affects their shipments | Being right early, with the source | WhatsApp channel → diagnostic → advisory or incentive work |
| MSMEs considering export | Headline vs Reality on an FTA covering their product | Specific numbers for *their* HS line | Readiness checklist → LEAP |
| Experienced exporters | Second Order and Draft Watch | Things they didn't already know | Peer discussion; advisory on new markets; they amplify the Desk |
| Importers interested in scrips | Direct outreach, CHA introduction | Clean, fast, documented transfers; due-diligence help | Repeat purchases; FTA duty planning |
| Students | Campus programme, Instagram | Clarity; career relevance | Follow; internships; long-term brand |
| Entrepreneurs | Market Door posts; Instagram | Data over hype | LEAP → first-shipment support |
| College audiences (institutions) | Offline programme track record | Structured curriculum; real-time case material | Annual campus contracts |
