# Content architecture

The minimum set that achieves the brand objective is **four categories**:

- **Signal** — the reputation engine.
- **Explain** — turns attention into understanding.
- **Proof** — turns understanding into trust.
- **Offer** — turns trust into revenue, sparingly.

Inside them sit recurring franchises. Readers learn to recognise them by name.

### Removed on purpose

- **Motivational and founder-journey posts** — except a rare, genuinely instructive story.
- **Festival greetings** as standalone posts.
- **"Export product ideas" listicles** — replaced by Market Door.
- **News round-ups without an angle** — the Saturday Brief is the only round-up, and every item in it carries a "so what".
- **Generic tips** ("5 tips to find buyers") — they commoditise the brand.


### The Angle Engine: eight questions that turn news into a Maverick Minds post

1. **Who pays and who gains?** Follow the money through the value chain.
2. **Which HS lines?** Name them. If you can't, the story isn't ready.
3. **Which Indian exposure?** The value of India's exports of those lines to that market. Source it.
4. **Which competitor country moves?** Relative change matters more than absolute change.
5. **Which cluster feels it first?** Tirupur, Karur, Coimbatore, Ambur, Tuticorin…
6. **What's the deadline or window?** When does it take effect, and when does it bite?
7. **What does the headline get wrong or leave out?**
8. **What should an exporter do this week?** If there's no action, it's a watchlist item, not a post.
