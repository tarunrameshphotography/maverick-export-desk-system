# Instagram strategy

**Role:** Instagram is the *explanation and trust* channel. It turns trade mechanics into something a first-generation business owner understands in a minute, in Tamil and English. It is LEAP's lead source. It is not the reputation engine, and it should never be a LinkedIn copy.


> **CHALLENGE · SHOULD INSTAGRAM MATTER MORE THAN LINKEDIN?**
>
> **For LEAP lead volume, possibly yes. For the brand and for high-value revenue, no.** Advisory and incentive clients decide on LinkedIn, WhatsApp and referrals. Run Instagram at a lower cadence (3–4 posts a week) with a separate content plan, and judge it on LEAP pipeline metrics, not on follower count.

| Decision | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Formats | About 60% Reels (45–75 s, founder on camera, captions burned in); about 40% carousels (7–10 slides, built to be saved). Reels bring discovery; carousels convert followers. |
| Language | Primarily Tamil voice-over with English on-screen text, plus English-only editions of the best pieces. Regional-language intelligence is close to empty territory. |
| Founder on video | Yes. Trust in export education is trust in a person, and the audience is on guard against scams. A real face at a real desk works better than stock footage. |
| Visual identity | "The document on the desk". Recurring overhead shots of the actual paper (shipping bill, BL, LC, a notification) with a hand pointing to the line that matters. Brand crimson for the highlight, charcoal for text, one condensed sans. No stock cityscapes, no money imagery, no container-ship clichés. |
| Lead generation | Comment a keyword → automated DM with a free resource (e.g. *Export Readiness Checklist*) → WhatsApp → invitation to a free live "Export Reality Check" → LEAP |
| Signals to optimise | Sends (DM shares), saves, watch-through. Third-party 2026 reporting says Instagram leadership has confirmed watch time, sends-per-reach and likes-per-reach as major signals, and that original content is favoured over reposts. Verify against your own Insights. |


### Instagram series


#### Paperwork, Decoded

One export document a week: what it is, the one line people get wrong, and what that line costs them.


#### Is This Real?

Anatomy of export scams: fake LCs, fake "buyer" advance-fee requests, fake agents. Protective, highly shareable, builds trust with the most wary segment.


#### 60-Second Desk

The week's best LinkedIn intelligence story, rebuilt for a beginner in one visual metaphor (a map, an invoice, a shipping route).


#### One Word

A trade term through a real current example (e.g. "war-risk surcharge" through the Hormuz situation).


#### Cluster Stories

Short documentary visits to exporting units in Tamil Nadu clusters. Only with consent, never implying income outcomes.


#### Reality Check

Myths about exporting answered honestly, including "who this isn't for".


### What not to post on Instagram

- Screenshots of notifications or dense infographics. They don't read on a phone.
- Scrip rates or "sell your scrip" promotions. Use WhatsApp Business and direct outreach.
- Income claims, earnings screenshots, "₹X lakh a month" hooks. Beyond the trust damage, these create advertising-policy risk.
- "Top 10 products to export" listicles.
- LinkedIn text pasted into a caption.
- Geopolitical hot takes. Keep conflict topics to their practical shipping and payment consequences.
