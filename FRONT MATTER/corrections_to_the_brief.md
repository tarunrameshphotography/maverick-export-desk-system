# Corrections and additions to the brief

The brief asked me to correct anything technically incomplete. These points matter for both content accuracy and the scrip business. Where I relied on secondary sources, the item carries a Verify label.

| Brief says / implies | What research shows | Label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scrips are "traded" | RoDTEP and RoSCTL credit is issued as an **e-scrip in an electronic duty-credit ledger on ICEGATE**. Transfer is IEC-to-IEC. The transferor enters the transferee's ICEGATE ID and IEC, and **both sides confirm by OTP**. There is no exchange: price is negotiated privately, typically a small discount to face value (secondary sources cite roughly 2–4%). _(ICEGATE Advisory 06/2021 (rev. 5 Jan 2024); pricing from market blogs, not official)_ | [FACT] [VERIFY] |
| Importers use scrips toward "applicable import-duty obligations" | Narrower than that. Duty credit pays only **duty leviable under the First Schedule to the Customs Tariff Act, 1975, i.e. Basic Customs Duty**. It cannot pay IGST or other levies. So **scrip demand is tied to how much BCD importers pay.** Every FTA that cuts BCD (UK CETA in force **15 JUL 2026**; the EU FTA to follow) and every Budget BCD cut shrinks that demand over time. _(Notification 24/2023-Customs (N.T.); ICEGATE advisory)_ | [FACT] [INTERPRETATION] |
| Scheme is a stable service line | RoDTEP has run on short extensions:Notification 60/2025-26 **halved rates and value caps** on **23 FEB 2026**, except HS chapters 01–24.Notification 66/2025-26 **restored** them on **23 MAR 2026**, citing the maritime situation.Notification 74/2025-26 **extended the scheme to **30 SEP 2026**** for DTA, AA, SEZ and EOU exports.FY27 allocation was cut to **₹10,000 crore** from ₹18,233 crore. RoSCTL is extended to **30 SEP 2026** "or until approval of the Scheme for the 16th Finance Commission cycle, whichever is earlier". **I found no announcement on what follows 30 September** as of 14 September. | [FACT] [VERIFY] |
| Scrips are valid for a year | Secondary sources report validity was changed from 1 year to **2 years from creation in the ledger** by a CBIC amendment to the Electronic Duty Credit Ledger Regulations (Sept 2022). Unused credit lapses after that. | [VERIFY] |
| Buyer risk is low | The **14 Sept 2022 amendments** to Notifications 76/2021 and 77/2021 removed recovery from the *transferee* for excess credit; recovery now runs against the exporter. That followed Delhi HC W.P.(C) 11178/2022. Fraud-linked recovery under Section 28AAA also runs against the person to whom the instrument was issued. Buyer-side due diligence is still good practice: check the scrip is active and not suspended. | [FACT] [VERIFY] |
| "Export incentive" | Officially RoDTEP is a **remission** of embedded, un-refunded taxes: fuel duty, electricity duty, mandi tax and similar. The framing matters: US Commerce has treated RoDTEP as **countervailable** in several CVD cases against Indian products (e.g. paper file folders, 2023). So Maverick Minds content should never call it a "subsidy" or "free money". | [FACT] |
| (not in brief) Compliance | An **Annual RoDTEP Return (ARR)** is mandatory for IECs claiming more than ₹1 crore a year. Late filing attracts composition fees, and missing it suspends benefits. Duty credit is also conditional on **realising export proceeds** within the FEMA period, or it is recovered with interest. GST on transferring duty credit scrips is **nil** (entry 122A, Notification 35/2017-CT(Rate)). | [FACT] |


> **CHALLENGE · THE SCRIP BUSINESS**
>
> The scrip line depends on three things Maverick Minds doesn't control:
>
> - the scheme continuing past **30 SEP 2026**
> - its budget, now cut 45%
> - importers' BCD bills, which FTAs are steadily shrinking
>
> It should be run as one part of a broader **Incentive Desk**, covering ARR filing, realisation compliance, drawback and Export Promotion Mission support, not as the brand's commercial core. Part 4 covers why it should also be kept out of the intelligence feed.
