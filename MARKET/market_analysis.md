# Market analysis: the export content landscape

### Why 2026 favours an intelligence brand

[FACT] The operating environment for Indian exporters has become unusually volatile this year. The list below is itself the best argument for the positioning.

| Development | Status (as researched) | Why exporters feel it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US tariffs on India | The Feb 2026 deal cut the rate from 50% to 18%. When Section 122 lapsed, a Section 301 two-tier tariff took effect on **24 JUL 2026**, with India in the 10% tier. BTA talks continue: Goyal says India will close only when it gets a "preferential rate in comparison to our competition". A G20 trade ministerial opens **30 SEP 2026**. | Landed-cost maths versus Vietnam, Bangladesh and China changes with every step |
| Strait of Hormuz | Largely blocked since **28 FEB 2026**; reported effectively closed to routine shipping from **30 JUL 2026**; attacks continuing into September | Gulf-bound freight, war-risk cover and transit times. ICRA estimated about 14% of India's exports are linked to West Asia. |
| RoDTEP / RoSCTL | Rates halved and then restored; extension ends **30 SEP 2026**; FY27 budget ₹10,000 cr | Price quotes for October shipments |
| FEMA export regime | New FEMA (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026 take effect **1 OCT 2026**: 15-month realisation (18 for INR invoices), a unified EDF, liberalised set-off. The old regime was reset to 9 months on **5 JUN 2026** for the transition. | Payment terms, bank paperwork, receivable risk |
| India–UK CETA | In force **15 JUL 2026**. UK steel safeguard tightened; UK CBAM from **1 JAN 2027**. | New margins for textiles, leather, marine, engineering; new costs for steel |
| India–EU FTA | Concluded Jan 2026; Commission proposed signature to the Council in Sept 2026; entry into force expected early 2027 | Rules-of-origin preparation window is *now* |
| EU CBAM | Definitive phase from 1 Jan 2026; certificates for 2026 imports are bought from Feb 2027 | EU buyers already pricing a carbon cost into steel and aluminium orders |
| EUDR | Applies **30 DEC 2026** to large and medium operators; **30 JUN 2027** to micro and small. Leather proposed for exclusion. | Coffee and rubber exporters get geolocation and due-diligence requests |

[INTERPRETATION] In a stable year, "how exporting works" content holds its value for a long time. In a year like this one, a large share of the practical questions exporters ask has an answer that changed in the last 90 days. **Interpretation of change is the scarce good.** Very few players are set up to produce it.


### What the market already produces: saturation map

| Topic or format | Saturation | Why | Maverick Minds move |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "How to start an export business", IEC registration, "zero-investment export" | **Very high** | Core funnel of every mass-market trainer; big YouTube and Instagram libraries | Don't compete head-on. Cover only through a "reality check" lens on Instagram. |
| "Top 10 products to export from India" | **Very high** | Easy listicle; rarely backed by data; often stale | Replace with data-backed *Market Door* posts on one HS code and one market |
| "What is RoDTEP / Incoterms / LC" explainers | **High** (SEO) | Fintechs and consultancies (Skydo, Xflow, Razorpay, EximPe, Afleo, Rasp) own Google for these queries | Explain mechanics only when news makes them urgent (news-pegged explainers) |
| Notification screenshot + "BIG UPDATE" | **High** | Tax portals (TaxGuru, Taxscan) and LinkedIn reposters | Same document, but lead with the consequence and the action |
| FTA celebration posts ("99% of lines duty-free!") | **High** | Amplification of official messaging | *Headline vs Reality*: which lines actually gained margin, against whom, and under what rules of origin |
| US tariff headline recaps | **High** | Every business page | Relative-tariff analysis: India's rate versus competitor countries, per product |
| Foreign draft regulations (WTO ePing SPS/TBT), EU RASFF alerts, US FDA refusals, Federal Register trade-remedy notices | **Very low** | Requires reading foreign primary sources systematically | Core franchise territory: *Draft Watch*, *Rejection Watch*, *Second Order* |
| Cluster-level implications (Tirupur, Karur, Coimbatore…) | **Very low** | National voices don't; local trainers do beginner content | Cluster lens on every relevant story |
| Accountability for past predictions | **None found** | Nobody keeps score | The Calls Ledger |


### Overused claims (avoid all of them)

"No.1 export trainer" · "India's best" · "earn lakhs from home" · "guaranteed buyers" · "zero investment" · "100% practical" · "real exporter" as a trust badge · "Nobody is talking about this" · "BIG UPDATE" · "game-changer". Mass-market education sites lean on scale claims: one prominent trainer's site cites 730,000+ YouTube subscribers and 51,000+ students. That arms race is lost before it starts. Credibility has to come from *specificity*, not size.
