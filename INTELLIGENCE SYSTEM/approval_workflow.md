# Approval workflow

Approval effort scales with risk. Most posts need five minutes of founder time. A few need a second pair of eyes.

| Risk tier | What falls in it | Required before publishing | Approver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Green** | Evergreen and news-pegged explainers; Freight Pulse; Ask the Desk on settled questions | Claims table complete; analyst self-check | Analyst; founder spot-checks weekly |
| **Amber** | Interpreting policy, data or FTAs; Countdowns; Second Order; Headline vs Reality | Primary document verified; every numeric claim quoted; interpretation labelled; pre-mortem line | Founder |
| **Red** | Anything about incentives or scrips, duty liability, eligibility, legal interpretation, named companies, trade-remedy cases, geopolitics | All Amber checks + disclosure line (if scrip-related) + partner CA or customs-broker check for legal points + wording review against the uncertainty ladder | Founder + external check |


### Pre-publish checklist (10 items)

1. Primary source opened and its number, date and link recorded.
2. Every number traceable to a quoted source line.
3. "Effective" versus "notified" dates correct.
4. Interpretation, forecast and opinion signalled in the language.
5. HS lines, markets and clusters named correctly.
6. An action is present, or the item is moved to the Watchlist.
7. Disclosure line present if scrip, RoDTEP or RoSCTL is involved.
8. No banned phrases; no income or outcome claims.
9. Visual matches the text (no chart implying more precision than the data has).
10. Any forward-looking call logged in the Calls Ledger with a review date.


### Service levels and escalation

- Lead post approved by **10:30 IST**. If it isn't, the backup Green item runs, and nothing unapproved goes out.
- Breaking primary-source news (e.g. a scheme extension notification) can use a **fast lane**: a factual-only post (what the document says, a link, "analysis tomorrow"), approved in 15 minutes.
- **Corrections:** within 24 hours of detection. Edit the post, add a "Correction:" comment, and log a `corrections` row with its root cause. Review root causes monthly.
